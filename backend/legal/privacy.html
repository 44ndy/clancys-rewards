Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Clancy's Rewards app collects, why, where it is kept and what you can ask us to do with it. The app is developed and operated by Andy Aravena. Contact: clancysrewards.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google profile picture and Google's confirmation that your email is verified. If you sign up with email, we store your password only as a secure hash, never in plain text.
  • Loyalty activity: the visits staff record for you, the rewards you unlock and redeem, and the time of each one.
  • Device data: if you allow notifications, a push token for your phone so we can send you messages about your rewards.
  • Staff accounts: for staff, the scans and deliveries they record, so the venue can see who validated each visit.

We do not collect payment details, your location or your contacts, and we do not use advertising or tracking tools.

Why we use it

Only to run the loyalty programme: to identify you, count your visits, show your rewards, let staff validate them and let the venue see how the programme is used. The legal basis is the performance of the service you sign up for. Notifications are sent only if you allow them on your phone.

Google sign-in

When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. We use them only to create and identify your account, and we do not access any other Google data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in the European Union: the app's server runs on Render (Frankfurt, Germany) and the database on MongoDB Atlas (Frankfurt, Germany). Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, apart from these hosting providers, which process it on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Email clancysrewards.app@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie).

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
