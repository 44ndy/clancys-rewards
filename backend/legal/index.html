Clancy's Rewards

Loyalty app for Clancy's, Princes Street, Cork, Ireland

Clancy's Rewards is a mobile loyalty programme. Guests sign up with their email or Google account, show a single-use QR code at the bar, and a member of staff scans it to record the visit. After a set number of visits, a reward is unlocked and can be redeemed in the venue.

The app is available for Android and is being prepared for the App Store and Google Play. It is developed by Andy Aravena as a software engineering project.
